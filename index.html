Tune Tutor

Play the highlighted note. Each one turns green when it's in tune, amber when it's sharp or flat, and flashes red if you hit the wrong note.

Scales go up and back down. Keys follow the circle of fifths, so Next key takes you round all twelve.

Upload a .musicxml or .mxl file. You can export one from MuseScore (free), Sibelius, Finale or Dorico, or download one from MuseScore.com. Scanner apps like PlayScore 2 can turn a photo of sheet music into MusicXML. The first part and the top line are used, and transposing parts like clarinet or trumpet are checked at the right pitch.
